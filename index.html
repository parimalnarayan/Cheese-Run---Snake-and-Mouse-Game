Cheese Run — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 August 2026

Cheese Run — Snake and Mouse Game ("Cheese Run", "the Game", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile game published for Android.

Information we collect

Cheese Run does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, phone number, address, contacts, photos, or other directly identifying information to play the Game.

The Game uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase/Google Analytics, to collect limited technical and usage information such as app interactions, device/app information, and diagnostic or performance information where those services are enabled. This information is used to understand how the Game is used and to improve stability, functionality, and the player experience.

How information is used

Analytics information may be used to understand gameplay usage, measure app performance, identify problems, and improve the Game. We do not use analytics to ask you for sensitive personal information.

Third-party services

The Game may use Google services, including Firebase and Google Analytics. These services may process information according to Google's own privacy practices and terms.

For more information, please review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy & Terms.

Advertising

The current version of Cheese Run is not intended to require you to provide personal information for advertising. If advertising or another third-party service is introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated as appropriate before or when that functionality is introduced.

Data retention and deletion

Because the Game does not require a user account, we do not maintain a player account database for the Game. Data processed by third-party analytics services is handled according to those providers' policies and retention settings.

Children's privacy

Cheese Run is intended to be a general-audience game. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us through the developer contact information provided on the Google Play listing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page, with its effective date shown above.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to Cheese Run, please use the developer contact information provided on the Game's Google Play listing.
