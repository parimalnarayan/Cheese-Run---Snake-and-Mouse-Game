Our story

A little more about LV Life.

Founded in 2020, LV Life is the organisation behind Cheese Run — Snake and Mouse Game. Cheese Run brings a familiar arcade idea to Android: guide your snake, collect cheese, and avoid hazards along the way.

We believe a good game can turn a spare moment into a little adventure. Thank you for spending yours with us.
