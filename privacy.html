Privacy policy

Originally published: 16 August 2026

Cheese Run — Snake and Mouse Game ("Cheese Run", "the Game", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile game published for Android by LV Life.

Information we collect

Cheese Run does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, phone number, address, contacts, photos, or other directly identifying information to play the Game.

The Game uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase/Google Analytics, to collect limited technical and usage information such as app interactions, device/app information, and diagnostic or performance information where those services are enabled. This information is used to understand how the Game is used and to improve stability, functionality, and the player experience.

How information is used

Analytics information may be used to understand gameplay usage, measure app performance, identify problems, and improve the Game. We do not use analytics to ask you for sensitive personal information.

Third-party services and advertising

The Game uses Google services, including Firebase and Google Analytics, and may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. These services may process device, usage, diagnostic, and advertising information according to their own privacy practices and settings. Advertising practices may depend on the version of the Game and your device or consent settings.

Read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Because the Game does not require a user account, we do not maintain a player account database for the Game. Data processed by third-party services is handled according to those providers' policies and retention settings. For a privacy question or request, contact devslazyvisionlife@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Cheese Run is intended to be a general-audience game. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be available here and on our dedicated privacy policy page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to Cheese Run, email devslazyvisionlife@gmail.com.
